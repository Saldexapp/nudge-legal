Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Nudge is developed and operated by Saldex. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what rights you have.

What we collect

When you create an account, we store:

  • Your email address
  • Your username
  • Your chosen avatar
  • Your chosen language

While you use the app, we also store:

  • Your streak and your best streak
  • Your XP, in total and per topic
  • The badges you have earned
  • Which question sets you have seen, so you don't get the same ones again
  • How many lessons you have taken today, so we can apply the daily limit
  • Your settings for sound, vibration and notifications
  • Whether you have an active Premium subscription

Your password is never stored in readable form. It is handled in encrypted form by our login provider.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos or any other information from your phone.

Why we process this information

We process your information in order to provide the app: to create and secure your account, to keep your progress across devices, to send you necessary emails, and to apply the difference between free and paid access. The legal basis is performance of the agreement between you and us.

Who processes information for us

We use the following providers:

Supabase handles login and the database. Your information is stored on servers in Ireland, within the EU.

Anthropic generates the questions in the app. We never send personal information to Anthropic. Only the topic and the language are sent — never who you are, what you answered, or how you are doing. If you type your own topic instead of choosing one from the list, that text is sent to Anthropic the same way — avoid typing personal information as a topic.

Resend sends emails to you, such as account confirmation and password reset. They only receive your email address.

Sentry monitors the app for technical errors and crashes, so we can find and fix problems. When an error occurs, Sentry may receive technical information such as device type, operating system version, and details about what went wrong in the app. This data is hosted in the European Union. Sentry does not receive your account credentials or the content of your questions and answers.

Google Play handles payment if you buy Premium. We never receive your card details. Google is a separate controller for your payment, and their own privacy policy applies to that part.

Notifications

The app can send you reminders if you turn them on. Notifications are created locally on your phone and are not sent through our servers. You can turn them off in the app's settings.

Information kept only on your phone

If you turn on biometric login, that setting is stored only on your device. We never receive your fingerprint or face data — your phone simply confirms to the app that it is you.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete your information. Some technical log files may remain for a short period afterwards before being deleted automatically.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Have incorrect information corrected
  • Have your information deleted
  • Receive your information in a readable format
  • Object to the processing
  • Complain to your national data protection authority if you believe we handle your information incorrectly

Write to us if you wish to use any of these rights. We respond within one month.

Age

Nudge is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly create accounts for younger users. If we become aware that a user is under 16, we delete the account.

Changes

We may update this policy. For significant changes we will notify you in the app. The date at the top shows when the policy was last changed.

We currently use no tools to measure user behaviour. Should we add this later, we will update this policy first and describe exactly what is measured.
