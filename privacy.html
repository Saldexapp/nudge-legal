Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Nudge is published by Saldex, which publishes the app on Google Play under the name Saldex Studio. Saldex Studio is Saldex's publisher name on Google Play. Saldex is the controller of the personal data the app processes. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what rights you have.

What we collect

When you create an account, we store:

  • Your email address
  • Your username
  • Your chosen avatar
  • Your chosen language
  • When you accepted the terms, which version you accepted, and that you confirmed your age

While you use the app, we also store:

  • Your streak and your best streak
  • How many streak saves you have used in the current month (Premium)
  • Your XP, in total and per topic
  • The badges you have earned
  • How you want to play (Learn or Challenge me)
  • Which question sets you have seen, so you don't get the same ones again
  • Your answers: which questions you have answered, whether the answer was right, and when. We use them for “Practise your mistakes”.
  • Questions you save to your saved questions, and the notes you write for them yourself
  • When you took your most recent lessons, so we can apply the daily limit
  • Your settings for sound, vibration and notifications
  • Whether you have an active Premium subscription

Your password is never stored in readable form. It is handled in encrypted form by our login provider.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos or any other information from your phone.

Why we process this information, and on what legal basis

  • Your account, your progress, the features of the app (such as the daily limit, Premium, “Practise your mistakes” and streak saves) and the emails needed to run your account: the processing is necessary to perform our agreement with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Error reports and security: we have a legitimate interest in finding and fixing errors and keeping the app secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object to this processing, see “Your rights”.

Who processes information for us

We use the following processors, who may only process the information on our instructions:

Supabase handles login and the database. Your information is stored on servers in Ireland, within the EU.

Sentry is used for error reporting: it records technical errors and crashes in the app, so we can find and fix problems. When an error occurs, Sentry receives technical information such as device type, operating system version and details about what went wrong. Sentry stores this data on servers in the EU. IP addresses are not stored, and error reports do not contain your name, your email address, your account credentials or the content of your questions and answers.

Resend sends system emails to you, such as account confirmation and password reset. The emails are sent from the domain auth.nudgeitnow.com via Resend’s servers in Ireland, within the EU. Resend receives your email address and the email itself.

Google Play handles payment if you buy Premium. We never receive your card details. Google is a separate controller for your payment, and their own privacy policy applies to that part.

Anthropic: the questions are created in advance on our server with AI models from Anthropic. The app sends no information about you or your use of it to Anthropic.

Transfers outside the EU

Supabase stores the database in Ireland, and Sentry stores error reports in the EU. If Supabase gives access to information from a country outside the EU/EEA, this takes place on the basis of the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Resend is a US company. According to Resend’s data processing agreement, information may also be processed in the USA; this takes place on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (a European Commission adequacy decision) and the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Notifications

The app can send you reminders if you turn them on. Notifications are created locally on your phone and are not sent through our servers. You can turn them off in the app's settings.

Information kept only on your phone

If you turn on biometric login, that setting is stored only on your device. We never receive your fingerprint or face data. Your phone simply confirms to the app that it is you.

The app also keeps a copy of your profile and your choice of Learn or Challenge me on your phone, so it works without an internet connection. The copy stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

To save you typing, the app remembers the email address you last logged in with, on your phone only. The app never stores your password; if you want it saved, your phone's own password manager can do that. The address is removed when you delete your account or uninstall the app.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (Menu → Delete account), your information is deleted from the database straight away. Error reports in Sentry are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Have incorrect information corrected
  • Have your information deleted
  • Have the processing restricted
  • Receive the information you have given us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format (data portability)
  • Object to processing based on our legitimate interest

Write to us at kontakt@saldex.dk if you wish to exercise any of these rights. We respond within one month.

You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, www.datatilsynet.dk) or to the data protection authority in the country where you live.

Age

Nudge is for people aged 16 and over. If you are 13 to 15, you may only create an account with the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13. If we discover an account that belongs to a person under the age limit or without the necessary parental consent, we delete the account.

Changes

We may update this policy. For significant changes we will notify you in the app. The date at the top shows when the policy was last changed.

We currently use no tools to measure user behaviour. Should we add this later, we will update this policy first and describe exactly what is measured.
