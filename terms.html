About Nudge

Nudge is here to make learning something new easy and fun – a few minutes at a time, with short questions on topics you're curious about. The idea is that small daily nudges make learning feel like play, not a chore. The questions are made with the help of artificial intelligence and checked against sources, but errors can occur. Nudge is for learning and entertainment and does not replace teaching or advice.
