Delete your account

Last updated: 31 October 2026

You can delete your Nudge account and its data at any time. Nudge is made by Saldex (published on Google Play as Saldex Studio).

Delete your account in the app

  • Open Nudge and log in.
  • Tap Menu (top of the home screen).
  • Tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm with Delete permanently.

The account is deleted at once. It cannot be undone.

Ask us to delete it

If you can no longer log in, write to kontakt@saldex.dk from the e-mail address of your account and ask us to delete it. We only delete an account when the request comes from its own e-mail address. We delete it within one month at the latest, usually much sooner, and write back when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: e-mail address, password (stored encrypted), username, avatar and language.
  • Your progress: streak, XP, badges, lessons, your answers and the question sets you have seen.
  • Your saved questions and your notes.
  • Your settings and when you accepted the terms.

Error reports in Sentry do not contain your name, e-mail or account ID and are deleted automatically after 30 days. The copy of your profile on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.

What is not deleted

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions). Google keeps its own records of your payments under its own privacy policy.

To stop the free trial from being given to the same person again, we keep an encrypted fingerprint (a hash made with a secret key) of your e-mail address for 12 months after the trial started. It cannot be turned back into your e-mail address and is then deleted automatically.
